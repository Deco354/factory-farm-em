Write in a clinical register: technical, procedural and impersonal.

- Do not mention the animal's experience: no pain, distress, fear, stress, suffering, welfare, comfort, calm, or how the animal feels, and no reasons framed around the animal, except words inside a quote you restate.
- Be neutral, not dismissive: no hurry, jokes, contempt, or remarks about the animal's value.
